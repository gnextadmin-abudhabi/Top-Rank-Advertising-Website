## Tips for Best Results in Google Flow

1. **Aspect ratios:** Use 1:1 (square) for service images, 16:9 for hero, and 1.91:1 for OG image.
2. **Style modifier:** Add "photorealistic, shot on professional DSLR, sharp focus, natural lighting" to every prompt.
3. **Workers without head coverings:** Prompts intentionally describe workers in standard polo shirts without traditional headwear, reflecting the indoor workshop and office environment.
4. **Logo on uniforms:** Every prompt includes "navy polo shirt with a circular teal logo on the chest." If Google Flow supports image reference, upload the actual `logo.jpeg` and add "logo reference image" to the prompt for more accurate results.
5. **Location cues:** Mention "Abu Dhabi," "UAE," or specific areas to get architecture and lighting that feels right.
6. **Avoid:** Do not add "illustration," "cartoon," "3D render," or "vector" unless you want non-photographic results.
7. **Iterating:** If a result looks too polished or AI-smooth, add "slightly imperfect, candid moment, documentary photography style."
